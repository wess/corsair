# Refunds

A template for operators. Corsair itself is free software and charges nobody.

## Cancelling

Cancelling stops the subscription renewing. Service continues to the end of the
period already paid for — mail is not cut off mid-period. At the end of it the
account drops to the trial plan, which has a smaller storage allowance; data over
that allowance is not deleted, but new mail may be deferred until you are back
under it.

## Getting your data out

Before cancelling, connect any IMAP client and copy your mail to a local folder,
or run a transfer in the other direction. Mail is yours and no operator should
make it difficult to leave with it.

## What an operator should state here

- The refund window, if any.
- Whether partial periods are refunded.
- How long data is retained after an account closes.
