# Acceptable use

A template for operators running Corsair as a service.

## Not permitted

- **Unsolicited bulk email.** Mail to recipients who did not ask for it, in any
  volume, however it was obtained.
- **Phishing.** Impersonating a person or organisation to obtain credentials,
  payment details, or anything else.
- **Fraud.** Advance-fee schemes, fake invoices, business email compromise.
- **Malware.** Distributing it, or linking to it.
- **Harassment**, threats, or content promoting violence.
- **Forgery** — sending as a domain you do not control, or relaying through the
  service on someone else's behalf.

## Enforcement

An operator should say here what they do when a report is upheld, and how to
appeal. Corsair provides the tools — per-address disable, per-account
termination, and a message log — but makes no decisions of its own.

## Reporting abuse

Reports should reach the operator's abuse address. Include the full headers of
the message in question; without them a report is usually unactionable.

## Technical limits

Daily send limits are enforced per account by plan, and per address if
configured. They are a rate control, not an acceptable-use policy — staying
under a limit does not make unsolicited mail acceptable.
